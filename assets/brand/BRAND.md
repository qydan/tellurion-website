# Tellurion brand kit v1

The official mark is **A2 · Earth + Moon orbits**: the Sun, Earth's orbit, and the Moon's orbit around Earth.
Every file here is redrawn as clean vector from the A2 master (traced geometry, 93% pixel match; the rest is anti-aliasing).
Use these files. Don't redraw the mark.

## Colours (two, no more)

| Name  | Hex       | Use |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Navy  | `#0E1B2C` | Backgrounds, the mark on light backgrounds |
| Brass | `#CAA24A` | The mark on navy, accents |

Neutrals for running text are allowed (white or off-white on navy, near-navy on white). They are not brand colours.
For brass text on a light background, darken it to `#8A6A22` so it stays readable (4.5:1).

## Type

Wordmark and headings: **Instrument Serif** (Google Fonts, OFL). Body: IBM Plex Sans. Data and labels: IBM Plex Mono.
The wordmark in the lockups is outlined, so it needs no font installed.

## Rules

- One colour per mark: all brass on navy, or all navy on light. Never mix the two inside the mark.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the Sun's diameter.
- Smallest sizes: 24 px tall for the mark, 20 px for the lockup's cap height. Below 48 px, use the favicon files.
- Don't rotate, stretch, recolour, outline, add shadows or gradients, or close the gaps in the orbits.
- Motto: *Nullius in verba*.
