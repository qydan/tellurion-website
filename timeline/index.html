    Tellurion Referee at the Open Scientific Intelligence Hackathon

    In two days: a simulated pendulum with an answer key, a learned world model, and a referee that names why the model and the measurements disagree. Tested once on a locked set of 1,000 runs: the model's own confidence missed 25.9% of real problems; the referee missed 3.6%.
